About Doggy Day Tours

Your Trusted Partner in Professional Pet Care

Our Story

We provide a selection of services for your dog and pets from dog walking, to house sitting and feeding in and around the KT postcodes. If you are really stuck we can safely taxi you and your pet to wherever you need to go.

Our aim is to provide your dog with a high standard of good loving safe care. We realise that your dog is an integral part of your family and it's loved. We show the same degree of care and respect to your dog and pets as we would show our own, you should expect nothing less than this when you place such a huge responsibility of pet care into the hands of others.

We can cater for all of your needs. From an early morning pick up of your dog to a late drop off if your out at work all day to caring for your pet while you are away on holiday or perhaps you just need to go out for the day and your worried about your animals at home? We are here to help.
